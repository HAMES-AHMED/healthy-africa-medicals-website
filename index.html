ABOUT HEALTHY AFRICA

Engineering solutions with healthcare at heart.

Healthy Africa Medical and Engineering Solutions Limited (HAMES) is a Uganda-based biomedical engineering company supporting healthcare facilities with medical equipment and technical services.

We help healthcare teams source equipment, plan installations and maintain essential devices so they can focus on patient care. Our work spans dental, laboratory, theatre, radiology and general medical equipment.
